# Patient User Guide

As a **Patient**, you use LabManager to stay updated on your medical reports, view past invoices, and securely interact with the laboratory.

## Core Features

### Accessing Medical Reports
*   **Secure Login:** You log into the system with the credentials provided by the Receptionist, secured by rate-limiting and strong passwords.
*   **Viewing Results:** Once the laboratory completes your tests, you can securely access, download, and review your medical reports online.
*   **Historical Data:** View your history of tests over time and track any conditions noted on your record.

### Viewing Invoices and Bills
*   **Billing Information:** Check the status of your bills, view the breakdown of charges, and download PDF copies of your invoices.
*   **Payment Methods:** See which payment methods were applied to past transactions.

## Privacy & Security
Your data, including your phone number and test results, is scoped and restricted solely to the laboratory you visited. The system employs encryption, rate limiters, and strict access controls to prevent unauthorized access.
