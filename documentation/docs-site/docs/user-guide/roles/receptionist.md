# Receptionist User Guide

As a **Receptionist**, you manage the front-desk operations of the medical laboratory, interacting with patients, scheduling tests, and handling billing.

## Patient Management

### Registration & Scheduling
*   **New Patients:** Register patients into the system by entering their personal details, phone numbers (which are scoped to your specific laboratory to protect privacy), and any known diseases or conditions.
*   **Booking Appointments:** Schedule patients for tests and cultures.

## Billing and Invoicing

### Processing Payments
*   **Creating Bills:** When a patient arrives for their test, create a bill by selecting the necessary tests, cultures, or pre-configured packages.
*   **Applying Discounts/Contracts:** If the patient belongs to a contracted company, apply the specific contract or package pricing.
*   **Invoicing:** Generate PDF invoices and manage payment methods.
