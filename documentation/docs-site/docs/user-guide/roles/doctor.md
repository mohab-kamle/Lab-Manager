# Doctor User Guide

As a **Doctor**, your primary role in the LabManager system is to review, approve, and oversee medical reports. You focus on clinical interpretation and patient diagnosis, ensuring that every report released by the laboratory meets medical standards.

## Medical Reports

### Reviewing Results
*   **Clinical Interpretation:** Review test results submitted by Chemists, verify they are consistent with the patient's clinical picture, and flag any anomalies that require re-testing.
*   **Adding Clinical Notes:** Attach clinical notes, observations, or recommendations to a patient's medical report before it is finalized.
*   **Diagnosis Annotations:** Where applicable, annotate reports with diagnostic impressions or follow-up suggestions for the referring physician or patient.

### Approval & Publishing
*   **Final Review:** Once all test results are entered and verified, review the complete medical report for accuracy and completeness.
*   **Report Authorization:** Approve and authorize reports so they become available to Receptionists for distribution and to Patients for online viewing.
*   **Rejection & Resubmission:** If a report contains errors or inconsistencies, reject it and request the Chemist to re-enter or verify the results.

## Patient History

*   **Historical Reports:** Access a patient's history of past reports and tests to identify trends, recurring conditions, or improvements over time.
*   **Known Conditions:** View any known diseases or conditions on the patient's record, which may inform the interpretation of current results.

## Monitoring

*   **Pending Reports:** Track which medical reports are awaiting your review and approval, ensuring timely turnaround for patients.
*   **Dashboard Overview:** Use the Doctor dashboard to get a quick overview of reports pending review, recently approved reports, and overall laboratory activity.
